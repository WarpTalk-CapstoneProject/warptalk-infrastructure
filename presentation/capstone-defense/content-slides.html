Section I · Problems

Two problems, one
broken conversation

Teams pay twice: once for the language gap, and again for the pile of tools bolted on to close it.

Language differences cause communication problems.

Subtitles arrive late, interpreters do not scale, and the speaker's own voice — the part people actually trust — disappears entirely.

Multiple tools make collaboration more complicated.

Meeting, captioning, translation, notes and storage each live in a different product — so the record of the conversation is scattered before anyone can use it.

Section II · Proposed solution

A B2B platform,
six key features

WarpTalk lets an organization join the system as a workspace and run multilingual meetings end to end — from live speech to a searchable record.

1

Real-time Multilingual Translation Meeting

Everyone speaks their own language; everyone hears the room in theirs, live.

2

Voice Cloning

The translation is delivered in the speaker's own voice, with consent and a safe fallback.

3

Live Transcript & WarpBot Assistant

The conversation is transcribed as it happens and answerable by an in-product AI assistant.

4

Google Meet Integration

Teams keep the meeting platform they already use — WarpTalk joins it as a bridge.

5

AI Summaries & Suggestions

Overview, decisions and action items are generated the moment the meeting ends.

6

Workspace Collaboration & AI Credits

Subscription plans, shared AI credits and usage tracking make it operable by an organization.

Section VII · Limitations

What this release
does not do

Stated before the results, so the results are read for what they are: a scoped system, not an unbounded claim.

01

A latency floor we cannot remove

Translated speech lands a few seconds behind the speaker: the audio has to be buffered before it can be transcribed at all.

02

A validated language set, not every language

The pipeline is proven on the languages we tested end to end. The picker is deliberately not a promise about the rest.

03

No translation-quality score

We show no confidence badge, because the only number we have measures speech recognition, not translation. Showing it would be a false signal.

04

One mixed recording, not one track per speaker

The room is captured as a single composite, so a recording cannot be cleanly re-transcribed afterwards.

05

Voice cloning needs consent and a clean sample

A clip below the quality bar is refused and the meeting falls back to a default voice rather than a bad imitation.

06

Web and desktop only

Participants join from a browser or the desktop app. There is no mobile client in this release.
